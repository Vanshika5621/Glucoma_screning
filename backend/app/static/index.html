Fundus image research demo

Upload a retinal fundus photo to prepare for an experimental research workflow. A regular selfie of the front of the eye is not supported.

Choose a retinal image

Use a JPEG or PNG fundus photograph that shows the retina and optic nerve. Maximum file size: 10 MB. If you choose to validate it, the image is sent only to this locally running app and not retained after the request.

This demo cannot verify that an image is actually a fundus photo.

Important: This learning project is not a medical device and cannot diagnose glaucoma. No trained model is currently configured, so no prediction or confidence score will be produced. Do not use this app for medical decisions. Contact an eye-care professional about health concerns.
